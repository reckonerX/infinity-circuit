We're excited to introduce the Infinity Circuit: a regional competitive circuit for Marvel: Crisis Protocol covering the Carolinas and Southern Virginia.

The circuit runs across a full season of sanctioned events in three tiers: 

* **Shards Events**, open to any sanctioned store
* **Infinity Qualifiers**, six events each tied to one of the six Infinity Stones
* and the **Infinity Gauntlet Championship**, a season-ending invitational for players who've earned their seat.

Our inaugural season kicks off with the first Infinity Qualifier on July 25th at Mighty Meeple in Concord, NC. Doors open at 10:30am, Round 1 begins at 11:00am. Entry is $25. The winner earns the Power Stone and a guaranteed invitation to the Infinity Gauntlet Championship.

Register on Longshanks: <https://www.longshanks.org/event/33245/>

Full details on the season calendar, Shard Point standings, partner stores, and circuit rules are available at infinitycircuitmcp.com. For discussion and questions about the event, feel free to [join our Discord](https://discord.gg/GPVX3B6C84). We look forward to seeing you all on the circuit!
