Our first season is moving fast. Here's where things stand.

**Power Stone Qualifier - July 25**

Mighty Meeple, Concord, NC. The event is at 30/32 players and closes this weekend. If you haven't registered, get on the waitlist now.

Register: <https://www.longshanks.org/event/33245/>

**Atomic Empire Shards Event - August 8**

Atomic Empire, Durham, NC. Registration is open. Shards Events award Shard Points toward Championship qualification — every result counts toward your season total.

Register: <https://longshanks.org/event/36176/>

**Reality Stone Qualifier - September 19**

House of Cards, Winston-Salem, NC. Capacity is limited, and registration is already half-full. Don't wait on this one.

Register: <https://longshanks.org/event/36154/>

**Space Stone Qualifier - October 17**

Atomic Empire, Durham, NC. Registration opens soon for this 32-player event. Come duke it out for the Space Stone and a guaranteed spot at the invitational.

**Virginia is coming.**

Two stores in the Richmond area - Battlegrounds in Midlothian and Waterloo Games in Mechanicsville - are joining the circuit. Dates to be announced soon.

Full standings and season details at infinitycircuitmcp.com. Interested in hosting a Shards Event? Use the application form on the website to get in touch.
